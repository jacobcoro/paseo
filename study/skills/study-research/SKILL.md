---
name: study-research
description: Produce sourced market comparisons, product research and literature summaries with the available live search tools.
---

Use hosted live web search to gather original sources and compare evidence across sources. Cite claims beside their sources. Distinguish verified facts, assumptions and hypothetical personas. Explain uncertainty rather than inventing quotations, product prices or study results.

Provide the requested research report. Use document tools when a downloadable report is requested. Product reference photographs must come from actual product sources; generated concept illustrations must be labelled as generated.

Do not impose a design method, AI-adoption level or research scoring scheme. The student chooses how to use the findings. This workflow does not enable the separate ChatGPT Deep Research product.
